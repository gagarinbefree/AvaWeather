AvaWeather — weather for your location

AvaWeather shows the weather near you without making you search for a city. Open the forecast in your browser or use the desktop application on Windows, Linux and macOS.

Open the live weather forecast

What the forecast includes

  • Current temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, UV index, pressure and visibility.
  • An hourly forecast for the rest of today and tomorrow, including precipitation chances.
  • A three-day outlook with highs, lows, rain chances, wind, sunrise and sunset.

How location and language work

AvaWeather estimates your location from your IP address. It uses GeoJS to find approximate coordinates and WeatherAPI for current conditions and forecasts. If GeoJS is unavailable, WeatherAPI can locate you by IP. The interface automatically chooses English or Russian based on the detected country.

Download AvaWeather

For platform-specific steps, see the installation guide.

The desktop builds are available on the latest release page. Choose the installer or standalone download for your operating system:

  • Windows: x64 installer or ARM64 installer.
  • macOS: Intel installer or Apple Silicon installer.
  • Linux: x64 download or ARM64 download.

The browser version runs directly on GitHub Pages. It needs JavaScript and WebAssembly; the desktop version runs without a browser.
